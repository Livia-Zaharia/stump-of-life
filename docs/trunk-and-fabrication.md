# Real log with a fitted DNA cross-section

The proposed construction is a seasoned real log carrying a replaceable metal
DNA disc. The log supplies the body and visual tree history; the disc supplies
repeatable nucleotide geometry. A 500 mm disc with 5 mm holes keeps exactly the
same digital resolution regardless of the log underneath it.

Direct carving into a log is another visual treatment. A shallow 5 mm recess is
plausible to machine, but a sample coupon is needed to assess grain tear-out,
contrast and weathering. The precision and durability of a machined surface
should not be assumed to equal sheet metal. A laser would engrave the exposed
surface, rather than cut the pattern through a seat-height log.

Wood movement is the main mounting issue. Even dried wood shrinks and swells with
moisture; drying differences can cause end checking. Design the plate connection
to accommodate this movement, using a fabricator-designed support with movement
allowance, ventilation and drainage. A perforated disc does not waterproof the
end grain. See the [USDA Wood Handbook, 2021](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/62200),
especially moisture, drying and fastenings.

## Material and cutting choice

A laser-cut stainless steel top is the leading option to prototype. The
5 mm substitution holes in our 500 mm example are comfortably larger than
micro-engraving features. Cutting method and edge finishing still need to be
confirmed against the fabricator's actual thickness and process. As one real
fabricator's comparison, [SendCutSend](https://sendcutsend.com/services/sheet-cutting/)
uses laser as its standard route for thin metal, with different limits for
waterjet and routing. These are its capabilities, not a universal tolerance.

For marine exposure, ask the fabricator to assess **316L or a more resistant
grade**, smooth finish, edge deburring, cleaning/passivation and drainage. The
[Australian Stainless Steel Development Association](https://assda.asn.au/publications/technical-faqs/preventing-coastal-corrosion-tea-staining)
recommends grade 316 or equivalent as a minimum near the surf, with higher grades
for severe exposure. Horizontal, salt-trapping details require particular care.
Neither “stainless” nor “marine grade” means maintenance-free.

The current example's 294 holes and measured 1.87 mm minimum remaining web are
**unmasked raw-sequence design geometry**. Earlier experiments used a conservative
site mask and fewer holes. A clearance pass establishes neither seat strength
nor the appropriate plate thickness. Final ancient profiles will change the
pattern and require another clearance check.

Procurement specification to quote: one stable, sound log section with a usable
top large enough for a 500 mm disc, final seat height around 43–48 cm, level
machined faces, declared species/origin and moisture condition, and a mounting
detail for a removable cap. A nominal diameter alone does not guarantee a flat
500 mm usable face on an irregular log.
